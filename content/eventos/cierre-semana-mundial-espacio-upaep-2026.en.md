## Program

- **09:00–09:30.** Project MILVUS. Rooms T 407 and T 408.
- **09:30–10:00.** Project AERODESIGN. Rooms T 407 and T 408.
- **10:00–10:30.** Project MEVA. Rooms T 407 and T 408.
- **10:30–11:00.** Project GXIBA-1. Rooms T 407 and T 408.
- **11:00–11:30.** Signing of the Aerospace Outreach Pact and the protocol close of World Space Week.
- **11:30–12:30.** Water-rocket competition at Explanada C. High-school category and undergraduate category.
- **12:30–13:00.** Closing of World Space Week.

## Who presents

Each project is presented by two students. The faculty leads choose who presents and notify them.

## Presentation format

- 20 minutes for the talk.
- 10 minutes for questions and answers.
