The week's theme was **Rocket Revolution**. The museum day brought together talks, telescopes, demonstrations, workshops and exhibitions. Admission was free.

## Talks

- **12:00.** *The Moon is waiting: Mexican technology toward the New Space.* Claudia Patricio (ICN-UNAM).
- **13:30.** *Solar space missions, to the edge of the Heliosphere.* Eduardo Mendoza (INAOE).
