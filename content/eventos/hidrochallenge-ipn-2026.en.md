Science, engineering and adrenaline meet in a competition that pushes talent, innovation and a passion for experimental rocketry.

## Launch

- **14 and 15 October.** Stage III: in-person launch and evaluation of water-propelled rockets.
- **16 October.** Stage III: in-person launch and evaluation of solid-fuel rockets.

## Experimental rocketry course

14 October, 11:00–13:00, at CIITA Puebla. Capacity of 50 people.

## Prizes

Casa Díaz and Creality sponsor the prizes.

## Road to launch

- **30 May.** Stage I: first technical report (PDR) and video.
- **19 June.** Results for the teams moving to phase two.
- **20 August.** Stage II: second delivery (CDR) and video.

The official account is on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/hidrochallenge_ipn_oficial/). The challenge announcement is in [this post](https://www.instagram.com/p/DeUyS7gAOrC/).
