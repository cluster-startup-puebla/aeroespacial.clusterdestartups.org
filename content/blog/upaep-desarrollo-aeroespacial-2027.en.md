## National closing at UPAEP

[UPAEP](https://www.upaep.mx/) hosted the national closing of World Space Week Mexico 2026 on October 10. The gathering brought together academic, scientific, business and government representatives. The day's program is on the [calendar](/en/calendario/cierre-semana-mundial-espacio-upaep-2026/).

At the ceremony a commemorative rocket was handed to the university as a symbol. That act marked the formal start of work toward the 2027 Space Year in Puebla.

## Innovation and alliances

The meeting highlighted UPAEP's record in satellites: the university has two devices in orbit. That experience is meant to strengthen specialized talent and applied research in the country.

The 2026 edition drew participation from 27 states, 71 cities and 145 venues. More than nine communities presented work on CubeSats, experimental rockets and robotic explorers.

Héctor Silva, of Puebla's [Secretariat of Science, Humanities, Technology and Innovation](/en/secihti/), presented the five-axis [manifesto](/en/manifiesto/). The plan promotes science outreach, links between academia and business, and Puebla's connection to international networks.

The [Secretariat of Economic Development and Labor](/en/sedetra/), [INAOE](/en/inaoe/), [CANACINTRA Puebla](/en/canacintra/) and the NACE Automotive Cluster also backed the initiative.

## Working together on what comes next

Rosa María Cantón Croda, UPAEP's vice rector for research, said the university is ready to add its capacities to every sector. She repeated that open collaboration is how the field creates value.

> We are completely open. We have worked with government, with public universities and with private universities. What we always seek is to share the experience we already have in aerospace.

Cantón Croda stressed student mobility and research aimed at patents and new ventures. She called for follow-up on the agreements with the 2027 Aerospace Forum in view.

With this gathering, Puebla sets a shared agenda to turn scientific knowledge into regional and international development.
