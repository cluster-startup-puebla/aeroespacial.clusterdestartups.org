## A week to talk about space

**World Space Week** is celebrated every year from October 4 to 10. The 2026 international theme was *Rocket Revolution*: startups, universities and student teams designing and building space technology.

Puebla took part with academic panels, guided visits and outreach activities at universities across the state.

## What comes next

The week closes a conversation that is just getting started. The cluster [calendar](/en/calendario/) lists the next ecosystem gatherings.
