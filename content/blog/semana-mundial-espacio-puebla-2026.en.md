## Puebla at the national inauguration

[World Space Week](https://www.un.org/en/observances/world-space-week) is held every year from **October 4 to 10**. The United Nations General Assembly declared it to recognize what space science and technology contribute to people's lives. It is the world's largest space-outreach event. In 2026 the theme is **Rocket Revolution**: startups, universities, and student teams design, build, and launch space technology.

On Monday, October 5, 2026, **Rodrigo Yamil Álvarez Moisés**, president of the [Cluster of Startups and Innovation (CSI) A.C.](https://clusterdestartups.org/en/quienes-somos/), took part in the national inauguration at the Chamber of Deputies in Mexico City. He spoke on behalf of Puebla's innovation ecosystem.

We thank [Regenia](https://regeniamexico.org/), the Network for the Generation of Knowledge and Open Innovation, and its president, [Denzel Carrillo](https://www.instagram.com/denzelcarrillo0/), for the invitation and the trust that let Puebla speak. Regenia and the National Coordination of World Space Week Mexico convened the gathering. Deputies María Teresa Ealy Díaz and Herminia López Santiago inaugurated it. The broadcast is on the [Canal del Congreso México](https://www.youtube.com/live/svMB9LN9-rQ). Yamil Álvarez's remarks are at [this point](https://www.youtube.com/live/svMB9LN9-rQ?t=9476).

## What Puebla put on the table

There is room to build companies in the aerospace industry, and local startups grow when government, academia, and business pull in the same direction. On September 25 the CSI visited the [Aeronautical University in Querétaro](https://clusterdestartups.org/en/blog/visita-unaq-space-tech-2026/) to open collaboration on Space Tech startups. The October 5 inauguration carries that conversation into the week when the country talks about space.

## Where the week comes from

The declaration dates to December 6, 1999, resolution 54/68. International coordination sits with the UN Office for Outer Space Affairs and the World Space Week Association.

The dates mark two milestones. On October 4, 1957, Sputnik 1, the first artificial satellite, was launched. On October 10, 1967, the Outer Space Treaty entered into force, the first agreement among nations on the peaceful use of outer space.

In Mexico the week is organized as [World Space Week Mexico](https://sme-mexico.space/2026/). It began in 2009 with Universum, the UNAM science museum. In 2013 the Mexican Space Agency extended it across the country. Between 2014 and 2018 there were activities in all 32 states, and Mexico reached third place worldwide in number of venues. In 2024 the program passed 700 activities.

## Regenia

[Regenia](https://regeniamexico.org/) is a national, independent, nonpartisan network. It brings together science communicators, universities, institutions, companies, governments, and citizens so scientific and technological knowledge can circulate and be used. [Denzel Carrillo](https://www.instagram.com/denzelcarrillo0/) described it at the inauguration as a bridge among industry, government, academia, and civil society.

## The pact for space outreach

In that participation the Cluster announced the signing of the **pact for space outreach**. It took place on Saturday, October 10, 2026, at the [World Space Week closing at UPAEP](/en/calendario/cierre-semana-mundial-espacio-upaep-2026/). Invited to the signing are [INAOE](https://www.inaoep.mx/), [UPAEP](https://upaep.mx/licenciaturas/ingenieria-aeroespacial), the [CSI](https://clusterdestartups.org/en/quienes-somos/), and further actors still to be confirmed.

INAOE, the National Institute of Astrophysics, Optics and Electronics, is based in Tonantzintla and is already an [ally of the Cluster](https://clusterdestartups.org/en/alianzas/) in applied research. UPAEP trains aerospace engineers in Puebla. The pact places that outreach next to the startup ecosystem.
